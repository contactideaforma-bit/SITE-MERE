# Surface brief — carrosserie-roc-blanc/index.html

Scope: one-page Persuade surface (home) + mentions légales. Audience: car owner on a phone after a bump; job: get a photo quote or call. Proof: before/after, insurer handling, courtesy car. Constraints: light ground, fonts unused in the book, structure unlike any other mockup, mobile pattern unlike bottom app bar, fictional, never "Sam".

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is built on the French registration plate (plaque SIV). Every French driver reads that object daily: blue band left, white retro-reflective field, blue band right with the department. The page refuses the dark-garage-with-red-accent template and the split hero + icon cards.

OWN-WORLD: plate white #F3F4F0 ground with reflective sheen, plate blue #1F3F9E for bands, black embossed lettering, old rear-plate yellow #F5C400 as the single action colour. Components are plates: rounded rect, 1px black rim, 4 screw heads, band left + band right. Condensed plate lettering (Saira Condensed) for display and immatriculations, Atkinson Hyperlegible (signage face) for reading.

STORY: "Un choc ? Tapez votre plaque, envoyez trois photos, devis sous 24 h." The visitor types their plate, sees it embossed live in the giant plate, attaches photos, sends. They believe the shop handles insurance and lends a car.

FIRST VIEWPORT: one giant plate fills the viewport width: left blue band with "F" + 12-dot ring, white field carries the hook line in plate lettering and, below, the WORKING immat input (styled as a plate) + "Devis photo" yellow plaque button + call link; right band carries "13 · Marignane". Behind the plate's lower edge, the atelier photo starts the next section.

FORM: candidate 5 of 7 (plaque SIV), seed key 850d5adc, degraded roll (no challengers).

Signature interaction: typing in the immat field re-letters the hero plate live with auto AA-123-AA formatting and emboss. Motion grammar: one authored moment, letters emboss in on load with exponential ease-out; sections are static otherwise.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
