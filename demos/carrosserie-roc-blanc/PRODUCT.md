# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

static HTML/CSS/JS (confirmed: the whole book of mockups is static, deployed on Vercel, shared assets/css/base.css + assets/js/main.js)

## Users

Primary: a car owner in the Marignane / étang de Berre area who just had a scratch, a dent, a parking bump or an accident, on a phone, often in the car park right after the incident, wanting to know "how much, how long, who do I call". Secondary: a fleet or company manager comparing local body shops (inferred from the brief).

## Product Purpose

The site is a sales mockup made by IDEA (myeasydev) to prospect real independent body shops. It must make a prospect say "I want this for my shop". Success: the prospect requests the site. For the end visitor: get a photo quote in under two minutes, or call.

## Positioning

An independent body shop (carrosserie) that handles the whole claim: photo quote, insurance paperwork, courtesy car, paint booth, return in days. Not a franchise, not a dealer. Fictional demo business: "Carrosserie Roc Blanc", 4 chemin des Ateliers, 13700 Marignane, 04 42 00 11 22.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from Google Maps / search on a phone. Opening hours Mon–Thu 8–12 / 14–18, Fri 8–12 / 14–17, Sat 8–12. Prestations: débosselage sans peinture (dès 59 €), rayures & retouches (dès 120 €), pare-chocs (dès 149 €), peinture cabine (dès 190 €), choc & sinistre (sur devis), vitrage (sur devis). Insurance claims (sinistre) handled with any insurer, courtesy car on request.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- One page + mentions légales, photo-quote form (demo, no backend: `data-demo-form`), click-to-call, map embed, opening-hours status, before/after.
- Shared base.css and main.js (mobile nav, cookie banner, demo forms, `.reveal`).
- Must NOT resemble the other mockups in the book (Mérou editorial, Sushi street, Maracana photographic, Mairie civic portal); each site has its own structure, fonts and graphics. Fonts never reused across the book (used: Newsreader, Familjen Grotesk, Castoro, Hanken Grotesk, Gabarito, Onest, Petrona, Mulish, Zilla Slab, Karla, Lilita One, Red Hat Text, Marcellus, Figtree, Bodoni Moda, Work Sans, Italiana, Jost, Archivo, Azeret Mono).
- Mobile version must be distinct from the other mockups' mobile patterns (bottom app bar with central button already used twice).
- Must read as hand-crafted by a professional, never "generated".
- Never cite any real body shop or person named "Sam".
- Light background preferred by the client (stated earlier).

## Brand Commitments

Fictional name "Carrosserie Roc Blanc" (le Roc Blanc = local hill above Marignane). Voice: direct, artisan, reassuring, Marseille-area plain French, no corporate hype. Previous anthracite/white/red industrial look is discarded (client: "trop générique").

## Evidence on Hand

No real photos of the shop: illustration photography from Pexels with verified URLs. Reviews are synthetic and labelled as demo. Prices are demo values to replace.

## Product Principles

- Prove the craft: show paint, metal, light on a panel; do not describe it.
- Phone first: the two actions (photo quote, call) are reachable from any scroll position.
- Reassure on the paperwork: insurance and courtesy car are what people actually fear.
- Distinct from every other site in the book, in structure before style.
